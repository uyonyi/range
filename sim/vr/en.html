Flight mode — GPS: stops when you let go · ATTI: attitude only, drifts with momentum and wind · Manual: stabilised within the tilt limit · ACRO: no tilt limit (flips, inverted), thrust ∝ rpm², bottom stick = idle. Assistance — Full: auto heading, gate guidance, terrain follow · Light: gate guidance only. Feel — Beginner: speed and tilt limited · Standard · Real aircraft: full specs. Collisions bounce and cost penalty time. Nav aids — Guide: target markers + chevrons and stick hints · Target: ◇ and beacon only · Map: on the map only · Radio: radio bearings only · Custom: exactly what you picked. Your choice is saved as the default (Auto = by flight style: Training Guide · Easy/ATTI Target · Sport Map) · GD or V in flight changes it for that sortie only.

RC transmitter (USB joystick mode)

Connect the transmitter by USB and assign axes (throttle·yaw·pitch·roll) and buttons in RC setup. Set stick feel to Real (linear) in Options. In menus: D-pad/stick to move, A select, B back, LB/RB switch tabs. In flight, click the left stick (LS) to hide/show the HUD.

TASK ORDER

  1. Takeoff
  2. Ring → window gate → triangle gate → window gate
  3. 6-pole slalom
  4. Drops: Ivan → MIT (one drop per task; a miss adds +10 s and moves on)
  5. Orbit the tank once → tank drop (top attack)
  6. Fast return (boost) → tunnel
  7. Bunker: go around the side, enter the front window → scan 1.5 s → exit through a window (the left one or the one you came in)
  8. FINISH gate → land on the right H-pad

CONTROLLER SETUP

Controller setup · RC transmitter (USB joystick mode) or gamepad

STICK ASSIGNMENT

THROTTLE STICK TYPE

With a non-centering throttle, center = 'altitude hold', push up to climb, pull down to descend (Training/Easy). A wide band around center is ignored.
Assign a button function with [Detect], then press the button or flip the switch on your controller. Assigned buttons override the default gamepad layout.

CALIBRATION

VR controller buttons (Quest)

Sticks follow the stick mode (1, 2 or head steer). Inside menus the triggers, A and B stay select / back.

TUTORIAL

Explanation and practice for each obstacle · start from any lesson (completed lessons show ✓)

Each lesson circles the obstacle while explaining it, then starts you right in front of it. No timer or penalties, and payloads refill. Difficulty follows the menu setting ( 'Training' is recommended at first).

DEM → TERRAIN

Reprojects a digital elevation model into the course frame and applies it as terrain

Click the map to put the course centre there. Yellow square = high-res area (1.2 km), orange = flight course, arrow = downrange direction.
